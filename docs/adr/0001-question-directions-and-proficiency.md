# Separate spatial question directions

The primary spatial exercise is **name-to-location**: the learner receives an entity name and selects its location on the 2D map or 3D globe. **Location-to-name recognition** is an alternative that presents a location and uses searchable candidates, not free recall. The two directions have separate proficiency and spaced-repetition schedules because spatial recall and candidate recognition measure different capabilities, while search assistance avoids making spelling and transliteration the geography gate.
