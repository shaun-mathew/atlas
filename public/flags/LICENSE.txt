Flag artwork snapshot retrieved 2026-09-07.

All SVGs except SOL.svg and CYN.svg are unmodified 4:3 assets from
https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons/tree/086f7e97d657358203916dbe84f61c2bccaa81eb/flags/4x3

Filenames use the learning dataset's Natural Earth ADM0_A3 identifiers,
mapped to upstream assets using ISO_A2_EH from Natural Earth 5.1.2.
https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/v5.1.2/geojson/ne_50m_admin_0_countries.geojson
Flags follow this pinned artwork collection, not a live political-status feed.
Siachen Glacier (KAS) has no country flag and is intentionally omitted.
Inclusion does not imply political recognition.
